Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

At Spendly, we only collect the information necessary to provide you with an effective expense tracking experience. This includes:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and encrypted password when you register.
  • Financial Records: The expense entries you create, including amount, category, date, and description.
  • Usage Data: Basic information about how you interact with the application to help us improve its performance.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • To maintain and manage your personal account.
  • To generate your spending summaries and category breakdowns.
  • To provide technical support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To analyze and improve our services (in an aggregated, non-personally identifiable way).

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. We may use trusted third-party services for essential infrastructure (like hosting or database management), but they are bound by confidentiality agreements to keep your information secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please feel free to reach out to us:

  • Email: privacy@spendly.com
  • Address: 123 Finance Street, Bengaluru, India
